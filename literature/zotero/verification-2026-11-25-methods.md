## Notes on markers

The marker at l. 1115 cites a fractional logit with fixed effects (`feglm`, quasibinomial). Papke and Wooldridge (1996) is the cross-sectional quasi-likelihood estimator. Their panel paper, Papke and Wooldridge (2008, J. Econometrics 145(1-2), 121-133, 10.1016/j.jeconom.2008.05.009, seen in Crossref), uses correlated random effects, not fixed effects. Neither paper covers a logit with high-dimensional fixed effects, which can have incidental-parameter bias. Since the fractional logit is robustness only, citing the 1996 paper is enough, but the prose should not suggest that the paper covers the fixed-effects case.

Unverified items: none of the 21 listed works, the two ICC works or the four additions is unverified. Two entries rest on evidence weaker than Crossref and are flagged in their notes. `holm1979simple` rests on a zbMATH Open database record and has no DOI or issue. `bell2002bias` rests on a logged web search result for the Statistics Canada catalogue and PDF, and has no DOI.
