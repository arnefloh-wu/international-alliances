Inclusion criteria applied (`literature/search/protocol.md`, v1.1): include (a) unit-, function-, level- or individual-level integration, coordination, control, autonomy or staffing inside an MNE, IJV or alliance; (b) IJV organization, control or staffing; (c) geopolitical or regulatory change altering the organization or staffing of foreign operations; (d) employment-history data; (e) structural contingency theory; (f) organizational design across functions or levels, decision rights, control mechanisms; (g) effects of sanctions, screening, export controls, mobility restrictions or political deterioration on firms' organization, staffing or alliances. Exclude (a) whole-unit only, with no internal differentiation and no IJV control or staffing; (b) formation, partner selection or performance only; (c) non-scholarly; (d) language.
