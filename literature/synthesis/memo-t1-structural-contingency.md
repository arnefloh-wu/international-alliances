## What is established

1. Subunits of one organization face different sub-environments and need
   different structures. This is the core of `lawrence1967organization`
   (standard reading, to verify): functional subunits such as sales,
   production and research differ in orientation and formal structure
   according to the uncertainty of their part of the environment, and the
   organization performs well when the differentiation it needs is matched by
   integration across subunits. This is the T1 claim that carries the
   function and occupation-level design of the paper. No other corpus work
   states it at the function level within a single organization.

2. The same logic has been applied inside the multinational, with the
   subsidiary as the subunit. HQ-subsidiary structures are systematically
   differentiated to fit each subsidiary's environmental complexity and local
   resources (`ghoshal1989internal`), differentiated fit relates to
   performance (`nohria1994differentiated`), and contingency links between
   strategy, structure and HRM hold more strongly in localized than in
   globalized subsidiaries (`brock2007global`). Business-unit organization
   design is treated as a fit to international strategy (`roth1991global`,
   screening reason only). These are T2 applications (see
   `memo-t2-ib-frameworks.md`), but they are the only corpus evidence that
   contingency fit has been tested in international settings.

3. Contingency reasoning has reached IJV control, though rarely under that
   label. Task interdependence between partners is positively related to
   behavior control in IJVs (`han2019management`), which is a direct task
   contingency on a parental coordination mechanism. An "organizational
   justice-based contingency approach" relates parental control structure to
   parent conflict, conditional on resource contribution and monitoring
   ability (`barden2005influence`). Schaan's dissertation frames parental
   control as a fit between success criteria, controlled activities and
   control mechanisms, with staffing as one mechanism (`schaan1983parent`).

## What is disputed

1. Determinism versus strategic choice. Classical contingency theory is
   often read as deterministic. The corpus has no T1 source for the
   strategic-choice critique `[CITE: Child 1972, strategic choice]` or for
   the defense that organizations adjust structure after a misfit lowers
   performance `[CITE: Donaldson 1987, structural adjustment to regain fit
   (SARFIT); Donaldson 2001]`. The corpus offers only indirect material:
   parents adjust control over the IJV life cycle and those that adjust
   perform better (`lenguyen2009parent`), and control evolves with
   bargaining power (`yan1994bargaining`).

2. Single versus multiple contingencies. Whether a unit can fit several
   contingencies at once, and how misaligned contingencies are resolved, is
   the topic of `[CITE: Van de Ven, Ganco & Hinings 2013]` and
   `[CITE: Drazin & Van de Ven 1985, forms of fit]`. Within the corpus, the
   closest evidence is that the integration and the responsiveness dimensions
   of subsidiary strategy respond to different contingencies
   (`meyer2014local`), which supports treating two contingencies as
   independent, and that integration and responsiveness are pursued at once
   within one function (`chung2024exploring`).

3. Structural fit versus substitutes for structure. Normative integration
   (shared values) can work alongside or instead of differentiated
   structural fit (`nohria1994differentiated`), and strong culture is
   associated with flatter managerial hierarchies (`marchetti2026are`). Both
   point to equifinality, which the paper must acknowledge because staffing
   is only one of several integration mechanisms.

## What is measured and how

In `lawrence1967organization` (standard reading, to verify) differentiation
was measured by questionnaires on subunit goal, time and interpersonal
orientations and formality of structure; environment by perceived
uncertainty. In the T2 applications the contingency is measured by manager
surveys of environmental complexity and local resource levels
(`ghoshal1989internal`), and fit as matching or interaction with
performance (`nohria1994differentiated`, `brock2007global`). In IJV control
the task contingency is a survey scale of task interdependence
(`han2019management`). No corpus work measures a contingency from an
external task database such as O\*NET, and none measures the structural
response through staffing histories. See `concept-bridge.md` for the O\*NET
route.

## What is missing for this project

1. The interdependence typology that turns "coordination dependence" into a
   theoretical construct `[CITE: Thompson 1967, pooled, sequential and
   reciprocal interdependence and their coordination modes]` and the
   information-processing view that links interdependence to lateral
   integration mechanisms `[CITE: Galbraith 1973 or 1974]`.
2. The adjustment-to-regain-fit argument for H4 `[CITE: Donaldson 1987;
   Donaldson 2001]`, and the strategic-choice qualification
   `[CITE: Child 1972]`.
3. A T1 source on multiple and changing contingencies
   `[CITE: Van de Ven, Ganco & Hinings 2013]`.
4. Any application of structural contingency theory to a unit with more than
   one parent organization. None was found in the provisional corpus; this is
   bounded by the incomplete search (manual database runs outstanding).
5. A source that treats staffing or personnel composition as a structural
   variable in the contingency tradition `[CITE: contingency theory with
   personnel composition or staffing as the structural outcome]`.
6. Contingency studies that score task environments from occupational task
   data (O\*NET or the Dictionary of Occupational Titles) rather than from
   surveys `[CITE: occupational task data as a contingency or
   task-environment measure]`; see `missing-references.md`.

## Implication for our theory section

The theory section can open on differentiation and integration across
subunits (`lawrence1967organization`) and should move quickly to the
international applications that show the same logic inside multinationals
(`ghoshal1989internal`, `nohria1994differentiated`). Each hypothesis needs a
named contingency, and at present only the environmental family has
direct T1 support in the corpus; coordination dependence needs Thompson and
Galbraith, and the dynamic hypothesis needs Donaldson and Child, none of whom
are in the bibliography. Until the S7 manual runs are ingested, the outline
in `theory-outline.md` carries these steps as `[CITE: ...]` markers rather
than leaning on T2 works to do T1 work. The interorganizational twist (two
parents) has no T1 precedent in the corpus and must be argued as the
paper's own extension, supported by the IJV control literature in T3.
