## Purpose

The pre-analysis plan builds two static contingency scores from O\*NET 31.0 elements. `coord_dependence` (narrow set): 4.A.4.b.1 Coordinating the Work and Activities of Others, 4.C.1.b.1.g Coordinate or Lead Others, 4.C.1.b.1.e Work With Work Group or Team. `local_embeddedness` (narrow set): 2.C.8.b Law and Government, 4.A.4.a.3 Communicating with Persons Outside Organization, 4.C.1.b.1.f Deal With External Customers. Three citation markers remain:

1. `[CITE: a study that uses O*NET task measures as contingency, interdependence or external-contact scores]`
2. `[CITE: O*NET content model documentation]` (check whether `nationalcenterforonetdevelopmentonet`, `handel2016onet`, `peterson1999occupational` and `peterson2001understanding`, already in `references.bib`, are enough)
3. `[CITE: cross-country transferability of O*NET task measures]`

Do not cite `hardy2018educational` as evidence of transferability.
