# Manuscript outline (JIBS)

Working outline for the academic-writer agent. Paragraph-level detail is
supplied by `literature/synthesis/theory-outline.md` (theory) and the
gate files (method, results). Target length to be confirmed against the
current JIBS guideline; plan for about 10,000 to 12,000 words including
references and tables.

## Abstract (about 150 words)
Phenomenon and tension; "We examine..."; data with numbers (interviews,
IJVs, cell-years); two to four contrastive findings; closing implication.

## 1 Introduction (4 to 6 paragraphs)
1. Field's attention: alliance and IJV integration as an alliance-level
   attribute (integrated vs. local; dominant vs. shared control).
2. The puzzle from the interviews: senior management cross-border,
   operations local; technical functions mixed, HR and government
   relations national.
3. Gap: integration theorized at the alliance level; differentiated
   network reasoning stops at the MNE boundary; dynamics under
   environmental change unexamined. Gap type: theory does not fit
   observed within-alliance variation plus no synthesis across levels.
4. Research question and the concept of differentiated international
   integration.
5. Study overview: Phase 1 interviews, Phase 2 panel with numbers,
   identification via the focal shock.
6. Three contributions (research idea, last paragraph), each tied to a
   literature; one sentence on managerial and policy relevance.

## 2 Theory and hypotheses
2.1 Integration in international alliances: the alliance-level view.
2.2 Differentiated international integration: definition, dimensions
    (functions, hierarchy), three measures.
2.3 Why domains differ: coordination dependence (H1), local
    embeddedness (H2), hierarchy (H3).
2.4 Dynamics: environmental change as selective reconfiguration
    (H4, H5), with the focal shock.

## 3 Study 1: qualitative grounding
Sample and procedure (Saldaña coding, respondent codes), findings per
theme (claim, quote, interpretation, literature link), how the findings
define the function classification and the measures used in Study 2.
One transitional paragraph into Study 2.

## 4 Study 2: method
Sample (Orbis, Revelio, matching, thresholds, sample construction log),
unit of analysis, measures (formulas), shock, controls, estimation,
identification (event study, pre-trends), robustness set.

## 5 Results
Descriptives and within-IJV differentiation; H1 to H3; H4 and H5 with
event-study figure; robustness; exploratory findings labelled.

## 6 Discussion
Restate with numbers; three numbered contributions; synthesis
paragraph; implications for managers and policymakers, numbered;
limitations paired with future research; short conclusion.
